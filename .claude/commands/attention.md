description: Everything that wants a decision this week, worst first. Arrears past their notice steps, habitability jobs, bonds not lodged, inspections booked without notice, compliance not met.
---

1. Run `npm run property -- attention [--manager=]`.
2. Do not just print it. Group it the way the command groups it and say what each group means in one line.
3. The order is the order the risk runs in, and it does not change:
   - **Arrears past twenty one days with no Tribunal application.** Every day here is rent the owner will not get back.
   - **Arrears past fourteen days with no notice to remedy.** The file is not on a footing.
   - **Rent overdue five working days with no notice served.** The cheapest fix on the list.
   - **Habitability jobs still open.** No hot water, no heat, no power, no lock.
   - **An inspection booked with no proper entry notice.** Entering is trespass. Move the visit or serve the notice today.
   - **A bond taken and not lodged.** Twenty three working days, and it is an unlawful act (RTA 1986 s 19).
   - **Compliance not met**, then **smoke alarms not checked**, then **inspections past the cycle**.
   - Then the money and the housekeeping: owners waiting on an answer, approved jobs nobody actioned, vacates with no exit inspection, fixed terms running out, rent reviews available, reports and statements never sent, tasks overdue, owners nobody has rung.
4. End with the five things to do today and one line each on why that one and not another. Weight it by risk first and money second: a Tribunal application beats a rent review, every week.
5. If the list is short, say so. A short list on a Monday is a result, not an error.

Nothing on this list is done by running the command. Each line maps to a command that records what a person did: `/arrears`, `/maintenance`, `/inspection`, `/notice`, `/compliance-items`, `add bond`.
